CatLobby is intended to be a friendly place to enjoy desktop cats and interact with other players. These rules apply to player names, profiles, and social content such as Couple Space Love Notes.

Rules

  • Treat other players respectfully. Harassment, bullying, threats, hate, and targeted abuse are not allowed.
  • Do not impersonate another person, organization, or rights holder.
  • Do not share another person's private information, credentials, address, phone number, or private messages without permission.
  • Do not spam, flood notifications, manipulate reports, or disrupt the service.
  • Share only names, artwork, characters, and other material you created or are permitted to use.
  • Do not submit illegal, sexually explicit, exploitative, hateful, or otherwise harmful content.

Reporting

Use Report player on a player's profile, or Report beside a received Couple Space Love Note. Include a short explanation and do not include passwords or unrelated private information. Reports are sent to a restricted CatLobby moderation queue. A submitted report is not automatically an account suspension or content removal.

Review and enforcement

The CatLobby operator reviews reports, investigates the relevant profile or Love Note, records the decision, and takes appropriate action when a rule is violated. Actions may include asking for a name change, restricting or suspending an account, or taking content-related action supported by the service. Reports are handled confidentially and must not be used for retaliation.
