Community Guidelines

Effective date: November 21, 2026

CatLobby is intended to be a friendly, general-audience space. These guidelines apply to display names, profiles, messages, friend interactions, lobby activity, and other content shared through CatLobby’s social features.

Questions or reports: F21forreal@gmail.com

1. Treat people respectfully

  • Do not harass, bully, threaten, intimidate, or repeatedly target another user.
  • Do not post hateful, discriminatory, or demeaning content about a person or group.
  • Respect another user’s decision not to talk, accept a friend request, or use optional couple/relationship features.

2. Keep content appropriate

  • Do not send sexually explicit, exploitative, or otherwise age-inappropriate content.
  • Do not encourage violence, self-harm, dangerous acts, or illegal activity.
  • Do not share another person’s private information, private conversations, or images without permission.
  • Do not use names, messages, or profile content to impersonate others, scam users, or spam the community.

3. Use social and relationship features responsibly

Friend, text, and optional couple/relationship features are voluntary. Do not pressure someone to respond, share personal details, accept a request, or continue an interaction. A user may choose not to use these features. Keep all interactions respectful and appropriate for a general-audience app.

4. How to report a problem

To report a display name, message, profile, or other social interaction, email F21forreal@gmail.com with the subject CatLobby community report. Include the username/display name involved, what happened, and when it happened. You may include screenshots if they help explain the issue. Do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents.

5. Review and moderation

Community reports are reviewed within 10 calendar days of receipt. Depending on the situation and available information, moderation may include removing content, limiting a feature, warning a user, or suspending access to CatLobby. Serious or urgent concerns may be prioritized where possible. Sending a report does not guarantee a particular outcome, and we may not be able to share private details about action taken on another account.

6. Follow-up or appeal

If you believe a moderation decision was made by mistake, reply to the support email or send a new email with the subject CatLobby moderation appeal. Explain the decision you are asking us to review and include your display name.

7. Contact

For community safety questions or reports, contact F21forreal@gmail.com.

These guidelines are intended to explain the rules and reporting process. They should also be available inside CatLobby, and the email-report process should be monitored so reports can actually be reviewed within the stated 10-day window.
