Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

CatLobby is a Windows desktop companion with customizable cats, music-reactive animations, Cat Beats, and optional social features. This policy explains the information that may be handled when you use CatLobby or contact support.

Public project name: F21
Privacy and support contact: F21forreal@gmail.com

1. Information CatLobby may handle

Depending on the features you use, CatLobby may handle:

  • Account information: account identifiers and sign-in information handled by the authentication provider, plus the display name you choose.
  • Profile and preferences: profile details, selected cats or cosmetics, and settings needed for the app to work.
  • Social information: friend requests and connections, lobby or presence information, optional couple/relationship interactions, and text or other content you choose to send through social features.
  • App activity: information needed to operate features such as lobby activity, emotes, taps, gifts, and game-related or community features.
  • Support and safety reports: the email address and details you include when contacting support about a problem, privacy request, or user report.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, government ID numbers, or other highly sensitive information in a support email or social message.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide and maintain CatLobby, sign users in, save preferences, operate social and lobby features, connect friends, respond to support requests, review reports, protect the service from misuse, and address technical problems.

3. Service providers

CatLobby uses Supabase for backend services, including account authentication and storage of app data. Information processed by Supabase is also subject to Supabase’s privacy information. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages, which may process basic technical information as part of providing the website; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

4. Social features and visibility

CatLobby includes optional social features. Depending on your settings and relationships in the app, other users may be able to see your chosen display name, profile information, presence, or activity and may receive text or other content you send them. Do not share your home address, phone number, school, passwords, or other private information with people you meet through the app.

Some optional couple or relationship features may not be relevant to every user. You do not need to use those features to enjoy the rest of CatLobby.

5. Retention and deletion requests

Information is kept for as long as it is needed to operate CatLobby, maintain account and social features, protect the service, resolve disputes, or meet applicable requirements. Different kinds of information may be kept for different periods; CatLobby does not currently promise one fixed retention period for every data type.

To request deletion of your account or information associated with it, email F21forreal@gmail.com with the subject CatLobby data deletion request and include your CatLobby display name. Do not include your password. We may ask for reasonable information to confirm that the request is yours. After reviewing the request, we will delete or de-identify information we control where reasonably possible. Limited information may remain where needed for security, dispute resolution, legal obligations, or in backups until those backups are replaced.

6. General-audience use and safety

CatLobby is intended for a general audience. Because some features allow users to communicate or share content with one another, use the social features thoughtfully and avoid sharing private information. Parents and guardians should guide younger users in using communication features safely. Contact us at the address below with privacy or safety concerns.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the information handled by CatLobby. No online service or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CatLobby’s features or practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a new version is published.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or account/data deletion requests, email F21forreal@gmail.com.

This page describes the current intended handling of CatLobby information. The app’s actual data collection, database settings, and deletion process should match this policy before public release.
