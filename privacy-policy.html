Information processed

Depending on the features used, CatLobby processes a Supabase user identifier and authentication/session data; player display name and profile; equipped appearance and inventory; score/tap totals, achievements, quests, streak and playtime/activity information; online status and last-active information; friend/member and Couple Space relationship state; reactions and Love Note text with sender/recipient identifiers; and player reports with reporter/target identifiers, explanation, report reference, status, and timestamps.

The app stores device preferences and local statistics, including cat position/scale, theme, settings, cached catalog/artwork, and local session information, under the CatLobby application-data folder. Music mode analyzes audio playing on the device to drive local visual effects. When optional online music metadata lookup is enabled, a track title and artist may be sent to Apple's iTunes Search service to retrieve artwork or genre information.

Purposes and service providers

CatLobby uses this information to sign players in, synchronize the shared lobby, display player profiles and appearances, save progress, deliver social features, enable optional activity sharing, display optional music metadata, and review user reports. Supabase provides authentication and online database services. Apple's iTunes Search receives track metadata only when its optional lookup is used. If automated moderation email alerts are enabled, Resend and the recipient's email provider receive limited alert metadata (report ID, reason category, and submission time) to deliver the notification; report explanations, player IDs, and private Love Note text are not included in that alert.
